NoteLens

Smart Handwritten Notes Organizer

Transform raw handwritten college notes into organized, searchable digital study materials automatically. Uses OpenCV image enhancement, Tesseract OCR text extraction, and local NLP subject and topic classification.

How NoteLens Works

4 simple steps to digitize and organize handwritten notes

1
Upload Image

Drag and drop or select photos of handwritten notebook pages.

2
Enhance & OCR

OpenCV denoises and binarizes handwriting for accurate text extraction.

3
Classify & Detect

Rule-based local NLP detects college subject and topic with confidence score.

4
Organize & Export

Review, edit, search anytime, or download clean TXT and PDF documents.
